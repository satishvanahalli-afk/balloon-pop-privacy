# Privacy Policy

**App:** Balloon Pop: Learning Games  
**Developer:** bimzora  
**Last updated:** November 16, 2026  
**Contact:** bimzora.studio@gmail.com

## Information collected

Balloon Pop: Learning Games is an offline learning game. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, usage analytics, or crash reports to bimzora.

The app does not require an account, login, or submission of personal information.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences, such as sound and background music settings, locally on your device. These preferences are used to remember your choices and are not transmitted to bimzora.

You can remove locally stored settings by clearing the app’s storage through Android settings or uninstalling the app. Android backup and restore behavior may depend on your device settings.

## Children’s privacy

The app is designed for young children and their families. It does not collect personal information from children, display advertisements, provide social features, or request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Parents and guardians may contact us with questions about their child’s privacy.

## Advertising, tracking, and third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party crash-reporting services, or third-party login services. Game activities and audio run locally.

Google Play and Android may process information independently when you download, update, or use their services. Their processing is governed by Google’s privacy policies.

## Information sharing

bimzora does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the app, because the app does not collect such information.

## Support communications

If you email bimzora.studio@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and address support issues.

Please do not include children’s personal information in support messages. Support emails are handled through Gmail and retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
